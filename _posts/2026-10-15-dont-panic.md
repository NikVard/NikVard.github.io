title: Don't Panic
tags: [life]
---

The first note on a new website feels like it ought to be profound. This one isn't going to be.

As Douglas Adams wrote on the cover of the titular Guide in his masterpiece, *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*: **Don't Panic.**

It has turned out to be surprisingly good advice for a scientist. In the operating room when the clock is running, when three recording systems refuse to agree on what time it is, when the reviewers send a third round of comments: take a breath, check you know where your towel is, and carry on.

More notes soon, on papers, methods, and whatever else seems worth writing down.
