Philadelphia, open to relocating.

I study what the brain's electrical activity can tell us about psychiatric illness, and how to use it to steer neuromodulation.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Neurosurgery at the University of Pennsylvania, working in the Halpern and Scangos Labs. I develop electrophysiological biomarkers from human intracranial recordings (stereo-EEG, ECoG, local field potentials) in patients with treatment-refractory obsessive-compulsive disorder, substance use disorder and loss-of-control eating, with the aim of guiding adaptive deep brain stimulation, TMS and focused ultrasound.

I trained as an electrical engineer at the Technical University of Crete, studied biomedical engineering at TU Delft, and did my Ph.D. in neuroscience at the University of Bordeaux, where I modeled and tested hippocampal stimulation for memory disorders.

I am open to industry roles in neurotechnology and brain-computer interfaces. Write to me.
